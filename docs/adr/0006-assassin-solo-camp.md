# The Assassin forms a solo Camp that blocks every other win while alive

The Assassin is a solo Role, at most one per Game, whose Camp is themself alone: they win only as the last Player alive. Each Night, in their own Turn right after the Werewolves', they may kill any other living Player, or nobody. They have no immunity: the Werewolves can make them their Victim like anyone else. A solo Camp is the only version of the Role that adds something the Game lacks; a Village Assassin would only repeat the Witch's poison, and a Werewolf-side one would be a Werewolf that wakes alone.

This extends the victory checks of [ADR 0005](0005-lovers-camp.md). While the Assassin is alive:

- **Assassin** wins when they are the only Player alive.
- **Village** cannot win: it needs every Werewolf **and** the Assassin dead.
- **Werewolves** cannot win, even at parity: they need the Assassin dead too. Otherwise one Werewolf facing the Assassin would win at once, although the Assassin kills every Night.
- **Lovers**: an Assassin bound to anyone forms the Lovers Camp, since two Lovers from different Camps always do, and wins as the last two alive. As a Lover the Assassin could never be the last one alive, because grief always takes the partner.

A Werewolf and the Assassin left alone at nightfall kill each other and both die at dawn: nobody is left alive, and the Game is a draw. Left alone at dawn instead, the Day's Vote ties and the Mayor, always one of the two, decides it.

The Witch's Turn comes after the Assassin's, so she can save their target. She learns who is dying tonight, never who chose them, and her one life potion saves a Player from every attack that Night.

## Considered options

- **The Assassin is immune to the Werewolves (as in Wolfy):** rejected. It is a hidden rule players must learn, and the Assassin already gets a sure kill every Night.
- **The Assassin's Turn after the Witch's, so the kill cannot be saved:** rejected. Letting the Witch weigh two deaths makes her Turn more interesting.
- **The Witch sees who chose each target:** rejected. With Hidden Roles on, a labelled target would tell her the Assassin is still alive.
- **Breaking the Night 1-on-1 by Turn order (the Werewolves' kill first):** rejected. It would be a new exception; both took the risk, and the existing "nobody left alive" draw covers it.
